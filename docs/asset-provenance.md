# Asset provenance and redistribution

This ledger covers assets eligible for the public Gear Share repository. A new
asset must be added here with its source and redistribution basis before it is
published.

## Inherited Community Supplies assets

The following files are byte-identical to files in the public
[`The-Relational-Technology-Project/community-supplies`](https://github.com/The-Relational-Technology-Project/community-supplies)
repository and are retained with that repository's MIT license and copyright
notice:

- `public/favicon.ico`
- `public/favicon.png`
- `public/lovable-uploads/227f35f7-b905-464b-bba4-82edcb95f777.png`
- `public/lovable-uploads/hand-drawn-supplies-1.png`
- `public/lovable-uploads/hand-drawn-supplies-2.png`
- `public/lovable-uploads/hand-drawn-supplies-3.png`
- `public/lovable-uploads/hand-drawn-supplies-4.png`
- `public/og-image.png`
- `public/placeholder.svg`

The public snapshot manifest must recheck byte identity against the recorded
upstream commit. This ledger does not claim separate authorship or rights beyond
the upstream repository's license.

## Deployment-specific assets

Community-specific artwork belongs under `public/brand/private/` in the
deploying community's private repository and must not be added to the public
template. Public defaults use `public/favicon.png`.

## New assets

New generic or community-specific artwork may be published only when its entry
records the author or source, exact file, applicable license or permission, and
any required attribution. Screenshots containing member information, private
catalog data, provider identifiers, or hosted evidence are never public assets.
